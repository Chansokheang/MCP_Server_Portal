# DemoCorp01 Domestic Business Trip Policy (revised September 2026)

Document HR-TR-2026-03 · Effective 2026-09-01 · Owner: HR Team 1 (인사1팀)

## Article 1. Purpose
This policy sets the rules for requesting, approving and settling domestic business trips by DemoCorp01 employees.

## Article 2. Types of trip
1. Day trip: a trip with no overnight stay.
2. Overnight trip: a trip with one or more nights of lodging.
3. A trip to Seoul or the capital area may be filed as an overnight trip when the departure point is in the Chungcheong area (Cheongju, Osong, Daejeon). If the meeting ends within 4 hours on one day, a day trip is the rule.

## Article 3. Before the trip: the trip plan (출장계획서)
1. Every trip must be filed as a trip plan and approved before departure.
2. Approval line: team leader, then department head. For Test Team 1 (테스트1팀) the approver is 김도하, Managing Director (상무), leader of HR Team 1 (인사1팀).
3. The plan states the destination, dates, purpose, companions and the expected means of transport.
4. A trip taken without an approved plan is reimbursed at 50% only. An urgent customer request may be approved after the fact.

## Article 4. Daily allowance (일비)
| Grade | Daily allowance |
|---|---|
| Regular staff (L1 to L3) | 40,000 KRW per day |
| Executives (상무 and above) | 60,000 KRW per day |
| Day trip under 4 hours | 20,000 KRW |

The daily allowance is calculated automatically from the trip dates. No receipt is needed.

## Article 5. Lodging (숙박비)
1. Regular staff receive a fixed 80,000 KRW per night. Any amount above that is the employee's own cost.
2. Executive Vice Presidents (전무) and above are reimbursed at cost up to 300,000 KRW per night; Vice Presidents (부사장) and above at cost with no limit.
3. Lodging in Seoul during peak season (October to November, April to May) or during large events is accepted up to 100,000 KRW per night. The reason must be written on the trip plan.
4. Lodging is evidenced by the hotel receipt or the card transaction. Without a receipt, only 80% of the fixed amount is accepted.

## Article 6. Transport (교통비)
1. KTX and SRT: standard class (일반실) is the rule. First class (특실) is allowed for executives only.
2. Air: domestic economy class only.
3. Express and intercity buses are reimbursed at cost; premium classes need a stated reason.
4. Private car: fuel at 200 KRW per km, tolls at cost.
5. Travel inside Seoul uses public transport. Taxis are accepted only with a reason, such as late night (after 22:00) or carrying equipment.
6. The standard fare for KTX standard class between Osong (오송) and Seoul (서울역) is 18,500 KRW one way. Amounts above that need a written reason.

## Article 7. Meals (식비)
1. Meals are included in the daily allowance; no separate meal expense is paid.
2. A business meal with a customer or partner is entertainment expense, handled separately, and may not exceed 30,000 KRW per person.

## Article 8. Settlement (출장정산서)
1. The settlement is filed within 7 days after the trip ends.
2. It is created from the approved trip plan; expenses not on the plan need a stated reason.
3. Amounts above the policy limits are split off as the employee's own cost at settlement.
4. The settlement uses the same approval line as the trip plan.
5. After approval, expenses are paid to the salary account on the 10th of the following month.

## Article 9. Evidence (증빙)
1. Transport and lodging are evidenced by a receipt or a card transaction.
2. Without a receipt, register the expense as other evidence (기타증빙) and state the reason.
3. Transport without evidence is accepted up to the public-transport standard fare.

## Article 10. Partner visits
A trip to a collaboration partner such as Bizplay states the project name as the trip purpose. Lodging or transport provided by the partner is not claimed.

## Frequently asked questions
- When can I claim travel expenses? File the settlement within 7 days after the trip ends.
- What if the hotel costs more than 80,000 KRW? The excess is split off as your own cost. In Seoul during peak season, up to 100,000 KRW is accepted.
- I lost the receipt. Register it as other evidence with a reason; up to 80% of the fixed amount is accepted.
- I travelled without a trip plan. Only 50% of the expenses are reimbursed. If it was an urgent customer request, get approval after the fact.
